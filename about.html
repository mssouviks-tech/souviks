ABOUT SOUVIKS

Nearly Four Decades Of Trust, Quality & Reliability

Since 1986, Souviks has been supplying genuine and aftermarket automobile spare parts to workshops, retailers, fleet operators and vehicle owners across India.

A Legacy Built On Trust Since 1986

For nearly four decades, Souviks has served the automotive industry with dependable spare parts, trusted brands and personalized customer support. What began as a local spare parts business has grown into a trusted supplier serving workshops, fleet operators, retailers and vehicle owners across India.

Today we maintain an extensive inventory of commercial vehicle components including clutch parts, filters, bearings, brake components and lubricants from leading manufacturers.
